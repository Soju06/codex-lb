The raw objective is transparent one-shot recovery for zero-event upstream socket loss. Negative controls are any response event, buffered reasoning/output, a second loss and an unproven continuation. The canonical owner is the existing HTTP bridge precreated reconnect path. Authority ends at a ready upstream PR; no other clone or live container may be touched. Completion evidence is route regression output plus published head readback. Example: reused socket closes 1000 before response.created; replacement emits response.completed on the original account. A close after response.created fails closed.
