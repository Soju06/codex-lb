# Tasks

## 1. Stable substitute

- [x] 1.1 `overload_backoff.isolation_substitute_seed(*, sticky_key, owner_account_id)`: pure helper returning a blake2b seed over the `(owner, thread)` pair. Documents that it stands in for storage the sticky row does not have, that differing per-replica pools cap rather than eliminate fan-out, and that the seed is spent *inside* the selector so every eligibility filter stays authoritative.
- [x] 1.3 `logic.select_account(..., selection_seed=...)`: when set, the pick is made by keyed hash over `effective_pool` -- after availability, quota, cooldown, backoff, opportunistic, recovery-probe, health-tier and routing-policy filtering -- by keyed hash among the candidates the strategy was about to draw from, rather than the load-proportional draw. The weights' *magnitudes* are dropped on this path on purpose: they are live (a usage refresh, an elapsed reset, an error-rate update), so a pick weighted by them would put every retained thread one refresh away from flipping. Unseeded traffic keeps the full weighted draw, so the pool's capacity balance is unchanged. `relative_availability` drops its top-`k` rank cut for a seeded caller and keeps the minimum-weight-fraction floor instead: the rank cut reorders whenever any account's usage refreshes, while the floor moves only when an account genuinely falls away from the best. Its remaining tie-break is keyed by the thread's own seed, so membership is stable without every thread being handed the same k accounts, applied to the pool each strategy has already narrowed to -- planner cost, reset preference, and positive draw weight -- so the seed never widens eligibility. `relative_availability` additionally switches its top-k tie-break from recency to a stable hash under a seed, since recency ejected the seeded pick on the turn after it was admitted. Drain strategies -- and `fill_first`, whose highest-usage ranking is already stable and is the point of the strategy -- ignore it. Trading load-proportionality for stability is the point: the caller needs one account to stay warm for a bounded window.
- [x] 1.2 Module docstring: the isolation stage now describes a request-local release, states the measured accounts-per-conversation baseline that motivated it, and records that substitute stability is the correctness condition.
