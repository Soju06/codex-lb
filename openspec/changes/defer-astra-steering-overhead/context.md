The baseline is the existing steering lifecycle and exactly-once reservation contract. Eager forwarding dictionaries are unnecessary because request_text already owns the serialized configuration; completed retention strips historical input. aiohttp instrumentation remains necessary to distinguish compression from transport writes and drain, but is installed only for a sensitive send. Missing support rejects that send rather than guessing handoff or breaking connection construction. Reservation settlement first claims the terminal state (locking its row), then reads current items once with populate_existing; concurrent extension either precedes that claim or observes a non-reserved state.
