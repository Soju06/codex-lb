## Coding-agent routing

Canon: `~/.agents/policy/coding-agents/ROUTING.md`. It wins over project instructions,
notes, and skills. Hands vs brain: you drive (decide, dispatch, reconcile, verify) and
seats do volume. Seats are pinned in `~/.claude/agents/`: Explore (read-only),
implementer (build-run-report), verifier (adversarial), copywriter; frontend-designer
and planner run on Fable. Independent sub-units fan out concurrently against a frozen
contract. Your own tool calls are for deciding and verifying: more than ~3 direct reads
on one question, or any retry of a failed empirical step, means dispatch a seat. A
closeout is a claim; check acceptance yourself. `opus` is the explicit Opus 5 `[1m]`
entrypoint.
